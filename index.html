Three generations of electricians

D&K Electric was started by our family and it is still run by our family. Three generations of us have pulled wire for a living, and what the first one learned the hard way, the third one still does the right way.

No call centers, no rotating crews, no one you have never met walking through your house. We quote the job before we start it, we clean up after ourselves, and we tell you honestly when something can wait and when it cannot. Most of our work comes from neighbors telling neighbors, and we would like to keep it that way.

What we do

Panel upgrades
Service changes, breaker replacement, and getting an old fuse box up to current code.
Rewiring and repairs
Knob and tube or aluminum replacement, damaged runs, and additions to existing circuits.
Troubleshooting
Breakers that keep tripping, dead outlets, flickering lights, and burning smells. Call us before it gets worse.
Lighting and fans
Recessed lighting, ceiling fans, under cabinet lighting, and exterior and security lights.
Outlets and switches
New circuits, GFCI and AFCI protection, dedicated appliance lines, and dimmer installs.
EV chargers
Level 2 charger installs sized to your panel, including the permit and inspection.
Generator hookups
Transfer switches and inlets so you are ready before the next storm rolls through.
Remodels and new work
Kitchens, baths, basements, garages, shops, and small commercial build outs.
